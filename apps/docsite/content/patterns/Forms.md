## Choose and label controls

Use the [Field guidance](../components/Field.md) for labels, hints, and validation.
For selection controls, read the [Dropdown guidance](../components/Dropdown.md)
before choosing an inline label, external label, or placeholder.

Placeholder text is not a replacement for a field label. Keep labels and
validation accessible, and preserve keyboard behavior.
